title: "Code for: Quantification of Ghana's historical mining expansion"
abstract: >-
  Google Earth Engine and R code to back-cast annual mining footprints for
  Ghana, 2000-2019. It builds 3-year dry-season Landsat medoid composites,
  trains a national random forest on manually labelled points from 2019,
  validates it with spatially blocked cross-validation, applies backward
  transition rules, estimates uncertainty with Monte Carlo draws from a
  bootstrapped ensemble, and compares mining onset with an independent
  convolutional neural network and industry statistics.
