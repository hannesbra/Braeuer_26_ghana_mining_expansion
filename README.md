These files replicate my quantification of Ghana's historical mining expansion.

Besides my Latek files, you will find key figures under \figures, and Gee and R scripts with respective Readmes under \scripts.
\Data contains the Maus et al (2022) mining polygons subset for Ghana, and my final training data points. 

NOTE: The annual mining footprint rasters are uploaded to Zenodo.
